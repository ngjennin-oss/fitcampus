Welcome to FitCampus

College is busy. Between classes, work, and studying, it is easy to skip workouts and grab whatever food is closest. FitCampus is a free guide made by a college student for college students who want to start exercising and eating better but do not know where to begin. Everything here is beginner friendly and built to fit a real class schedule and a student budget.
