Beginner Workouts

Short plans that fit between classes, in the gym or your dorm room.

Your First Week

New to exercise? Start here. This one-week schedule mixes strength, cardio, and rest so your body can adjust without getting sore or burned out. Every workout takes 30 minutes or less, so you can fit it in before class, between classes, or after your last lecture. Repeat the week for four weeks, then add a few more reps or a few more minutes each week.

Dorm Workout (No Equipment)

Do each move for the reps listed, rest one minute, and repeat the circuit three times.

  • 10 bodyweight squats
  • 8 push-ups (knees on the floor is fine)
  • 10 reverse lunges per leg
  • 20-second plank
  • 15 glute bridges

Gym Starter Routine

Use the machines at your campus rec center. Pick a weight you can lift 10 times with good form.

  • Leg press: 3 sets of 10
  • Chest press machine: 3 sets of 10
  • Seated cable row: 3 sets of 10
  • Lat pulldown: 3 sets of 10
  • 5 minutes on the bike to cool down

Tips for Sticking With It

  • Put your workouts in your calendar like a class so you do not skip them.
  • Warm up for five minutes before every workout and stretch afterward.
  • Find a workout buddy from your dorm or class to keep each other on track.
  • Most campus rec centers are free for students, so use the equipment you already pay for.
